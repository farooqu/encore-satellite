Known evidence and uncertainty
------------------------------
No firmware-specific license grant or preferred source for modifying this FPGA
program was established in the inspected original archive/notices. The archive's
Linux GPL notices do not unambiguously identify the separately loaded FPGA
program as covered. NOASSERTION records that uncertainty; it does not mean MIT,
GPL, public domain, a newly granted permission, or a finding of infringement.
If source-disclosure obligations apply, this decision does not waive them.

Techo5 Dot v1.0.71, LibreEcho Biscuit v0.14.6 and Kiosk Satellite Biscuit

Owner decision and limits
-------------------------
The project owner accepts the unresolved redistribution/source-compliance risk
for the unchanged hash-pinned binary, following this documented precedent, for
both dev and production releases. This is a project release-policy exception,
not legal clearance and not a claim of rights beyond any applicable license.
Decision record: https://ampcode.com/threads/T-01a1e785-14fb-7097-bd5e-409d3e48ae0f

Preserve this record and all existing notices with distributed kernel/images
and corresponding source. A retained immutable source kit predating the decision
must not be rewritten or relabeled; provide this record alongside it instead.
Re-review the exception if the firmware bytes, provenance, use or licensing
evidence change. Retain immutable release records and a withdrawal/replacement
path if authoritative rights information requires action.

This exception does not cover other proprietary firmware, vendor images,
Amazon wake-word engines/models, or a replacement FPGA program. It does not
waive any other notice/source/signing requirements, authorize publication or
automatic CI artifact uploads, establish stable support/hardware qualification,
or authorize installation, flashing or device changes.
